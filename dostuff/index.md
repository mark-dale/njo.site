# DoStuff!

**If you want stuff done... do stuff!**

DoStuff! is a friendly, flexible lists app for iPhone and iPad. Use it for shopping, packing, home projects, gift ideas, anything you want to get done. Lists sync between your own devices through iCloud.

## Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email [notjustopera@gmail.com](mailto:notjustopera@gmail.com) and I'll get back to you.

Some things worth knowing:

- **Lists not syncing?** Check you're signed in to iCloud on each device (Settings > your name > iCloud) and that iCloud Drive is on for DoStuff!. The status line at the bottom of the library shows what sync is doing.
- **Deleted something by mistake?** Open Recently Deleted: lists, groups and items are kept for 30 days and can be restored.
- **Moving lists in or out?** Each list can be imported from or exported to CSV from its settings.
