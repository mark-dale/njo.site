# DoStuff! privacy policy

*Last updated: 1 November 2026*

DoStuff! is made by Mark Dale. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version

DoStuff! does not collect, track, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

## Where your lists live

Your lists, items, notes and attachments are stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. This is handled by Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) service under your Apple ID. I cannot see or access your data.

When you share a list with someone, it is shared with the people you invite, through iCloud. Nobody else can see it.

## Camera and photos

DoStuff! only uses the camera or your photo library when you choose to attach a photo to an item. Those attachments stay with the item, on your device and in your iCloud.

## Third parties

DoStuff! does not include any third-party code, advertising or analytics services, and sends nothing to servers run by me or anyone else apart from Apple's iCloud.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a list or item in the app moves it to Recently Deleted for 30 days and then removes it. You can delete everything by removing the app and, in Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage, deleting DoStuff! data from iCloud.

## Children

DoStuff! does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
