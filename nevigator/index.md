# NEVigator

A map and information app for Narara Ecovillage.

Visitors can use the map to find their way around the village. Members who have been given log-in details can sign in to see more village information and services.

## Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email [notjustopera@gmail.com](mailto:notjustopera@gmail.com) and I'll get back to you.

Some things worth knowing:

- **Your position isn't showing on the map?** Check that location access is allowed for NEVigator in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. NEVigator only uses your location to show where you are on the map, and never stores it.
- **Can't log in?** Log-in details are given to members of Narara Ecovillage. Check you're using the email address you were given, and email me if it still doesn't work.
- **Visitors don't need to log in.** The map works without an account.
