# NEVigator privacy policy

*Last updated: 1 November 2026*

NEVigator is a map and information app for Narara Ecovillage, made by Mark Dale. This page explains what the app does with your information.

## Visitors

If you are a visitor using NEVigator to find your way around Narara Ecovillage, NEVigator does not ask for or store any details about you. It does not store your location. Your location is only used to show where you are on the map.

## Members

If you are a member of Narara Ecovillage and have been given log-in details, NEVigator uses your email address and password only to give you secure access to the village database.

- Your email address is stored on your device only, so the app can fill it in the next time you log in after logging out.
- Your password is never stored.
- No phone numbers or email addresses of other people are available within NEVigator.

## Location

Your location is never stored. It is only used to show your position on the map while you use the app.

## Services the app relies on

To work, NEVigator uses these third-party services:

- **Google Firebase** holds the village information and signs members in. Members' log-in details are handled by Firebase Authentication.
- **Google Maps** draws the map.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** receives anonymous technical reports if the app crashes (such as the device model, iOS version and where in the app the problem happened), so that problems can be fixed.

## Children

NEVigator does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
